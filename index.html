About Eco-centive

ECIR (Eco-Centive Innovative Robot) is a battery-powered robot that collects trash and sorts it into metal, wet and dry compartments. Users earn points and redeem them as coupons.

  • Less landfill: sorted waste can be recycled or composted.
  • Habit change: points make sorting worth the effort.
  • Real rewards: redeem points for gaming credits and vouchers.
  • Local problem: built for proper waste disposal and management in the Philippines.
  • Trash-to-Cash, upgraded: a modern take on the Trash-to-Cash program in Lipa City.

How it works: 3 sorted compartments

Wet Trash

+15 pts per item

Organic waste.

  • Food scraps
  • Fruit and veg peels
  • Leftovers

Metal

+30 pts per item

Cans and tins.

  • Aluminum cans
  • Steel tins
  • Empty and rinsed

Dry Trash

+20 pts per item

Clean recyclables.

  • Paper
  • Cardboard
  • Non-metal items

Why separate? Wet waste soaks into paper and cardboard and makes them unrecyclable. Splitting wet, metal and dry keeps dry items clean and raises recycling efficiency.
