// ABOUT & GOALS

Trash-to-Cash, rebuilt with a robot.

ECIR is a modern take on the Trash-to-Cash program in Lipa City. It targets proper waste disposal and management, one of the biggest environmental problems in the Philippines.

  • Local waste management: built for Philippine communities.
  • Smart automation: the robot collects and sorts by itself.
  • Less landfill: sorted waste can be recycled or composted.
  • Positive habits: points make sorting worth the effort.

// HOW IT WORKS

3 compartments. 1 simple rule.

CANISTER 01

+15

Wet Trash

+15 pts / item

Organic waste, food leftovers and vegetable scraps.

CANISTER 02

+30

Metal

+30 pts / item

Aluminum cans and steel tins.

CANISTER 03

+20

Dry Trash

+20 pts / item

Clean recyclables: paper, cardboard and non-metal items.

Why separate? Wet waste soaks into cardboard and paper and makes them unrecyclable. Splitting wet, metal and dry keeps recyclables clean.
